What this receipt proves

A payment of exactly this amount arrived in a Tari wallet at the time shown.

This receipt was issued by the wallet that received the payment. It cannot be forged or edited by anyone else, and that wallet can only issue receipts for payments that really arrived.

The memo shown is the one that wallet opened from this payment, and it is covered by the same signature.

What this receipt does not prove

Who sent the payment. This receipt names no sender.

That the money is still in the wallet. It may have been spent since.

That the payment was for genuine work. A person can pay themselves from a second wallet and receive a perfectly valid receipt. Treat this receipt as proof that a payment happened, not proof of a job.

PaidSeal checks receipts in your browser. This site has no server of its own, and the receipt data in this link is never sent to one. To check the payment, the page asks a Tari public node about the output, and that node can see the query.
